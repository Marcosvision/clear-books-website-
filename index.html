What we do

Practical bookkeeping services, explained simply

Choose only what you need. Most clients start with daily or monthly bookkeeping and add other services later.

1

Daily bookkeeping & transaction recording

We record your day-to-day money in and out so your books are always up to date and never a pile of paper.

2

Accounts payable & supplier reconciliations

We track what you owe suppliers and match your records to their statements, so you pay the right amount on time.

3

Accounts receivable & customer balances

We keep a clear list of who owes you, how much and for how long, so you can follow up on late payments.

4

Bank & cash reconciliations

We compare your records with bank, M-Pesa and cash-on-hand, and explain any differences we find.

5

Sales, purchases, expenses & invoice recording

Every invoice and receipt recorded in the right category, ready for reports and tax records.

6

Inventory & stock reconciliation

We match your stock records to what is on the shelf, helping you see losses, slow items and real stock value.

7

Payroll processing & statutory deductions support

We prepare payslips and payroll records and calculate deductions such as PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and Housing Levy, based on the rates in force and the information you give us.

8

VAT, PAYE & Kenya tax-record preparation

We organise and prepare the records and schedules you need for your tax filings. You or your authorised tax agent submit and sign them.

9

Monthly management accounts & reports

A simple monthly pack that shows how the business performed, with a short explanation in plain language.

10

Profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow

The three core statements: what you earned, what you own and owe, and where your cash went.

11

QuickBooks Desktop & Online setup and support

Set up properly from the start, or fix and tidy an existing file, with help when you get stuck.

12

Accounting software setup, cleanup & training

We set up your chart of accounts, clean messy data and train you or your staff to use the system.

What we do and do not do. We provide bookkeeping, reconciliation, reporting and tax-record preparation support. We do not provide audit or assurance services, and we do not act as your tax agent or represent you before the tax authority unless properly authorised and agreed in writing. If you need those services, we will tell you and can work alongside your authorised professional.

About us

About the Founder

I am an accounting professional with a background in Economics and Mathematics and practical experience in bookkeeping, financial reporting, and day-to-day accounting operations.

During my time working as an Accountant in a 1500 acre Farm in Kenya, I gained hands-on experience in accounts payable and receivable, bank reconciliations, invoice verification, financial and operational reporting, inventory records, asset management, and budget and cost monitoring.

My experience working in a farm environment helped me understand the importance of maintaining accurate financial records, monitoring expenses, tracking assets, and providing reliable financial information to support business decisions.

I have also been developing my skills in accounting software, including QuickBooks Desktop, QuickBooks Online, and Zoho Books, to strengthen my ability to support businesses with organized and efficient bookkeeping processes.

I started ClearBooks Accounting & Bookkeeping Services with a simple goal: to help small and medium-sized businesses maintain accurate financial records, understand their financial performance, monitor cash flow, and make more informed business decisions.

I believe every business deserves reliable bookkeeping and clear financial information, regardless of its size. My approach is built on accuracy, accountability, confidentiality, continuous learning, and a commitment to helping business owners spend less time worrying about their records and more time growing their businesses.

FAQ

Questions business owners often ask

How much does your service cost?

Our packages start from , and per month. The final fee depends on transaction volume, number of accounts, payroll, inventory complexity, reporting frequency and scope. You get a written quote after a free consultation.

Is my financial information kept confidential?

Yes. We use your records only to do your work and do not share them without your permission. You can ask us to sign a confidentiality agreement.

Can you work with me remotely?

Yes. You can send documents by WhatsApp, email or photos of receipts, and we can hold review calls. In-person visits can be discussed depending on location.

Which accounting software do you work with?

We support QuickBooks Desktop and QuickBooks Online, and can also work in well-organised Excel or spreadsheet records. Tell us what you already use and we will advise.

How does onboarding work?

After your free consultation, you send the documents we list. We review them, agree the scope and quote, then set up your books and start regular monthly work.

Can you file my tax returns or represent me before the tax authority?

We prepare the bookkeeping records and schedules that support your tax filings. Filing, signing and representation are done by you or your authorised tax agent, unless we have agreed otherwise in writing and are properly authorised.

My records are a mess. Can you still help?

Yes. Data cleanup and catch-up work is a common starting point. We review what you have and quote for the cleanup separately.
